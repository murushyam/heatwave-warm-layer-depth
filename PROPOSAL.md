# Full Research Proposal: How Deep Does a Heatwave Reach?

See README.md for the complete finalized proposal.

This repository holds the research design for multi-city warm-layer depth timing, heat-dome control, and the Physics-Constrained Spatial-Attention Network (PC-SAN) for 1 km reconstruction.

**Cities:** Hyderabad, Delhi, Austin, Prague, Seattle

**Key references:** Hotz et al. (2024, 2026), Lhotka & Kyselý (2024), Lakshman et al. (2025)
